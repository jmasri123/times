Prayer time windows

Finding your location…

Methods covered: ISNA (15°/15°), MWL (18°/17°) and Moonsighting Committee Worldwide (seasonal, latitude-adjusted). Asr is shown at 1× and 2× (Hanafi) shadow, Dhuhr and Maghrib include Moonsighting’s small safety minutes, and Isha ends at midnight or dawn. Asr is also discouraged once the sun yellows, which is not shown. This is a planning aid, not a fatwa; follow your local scholar or mosque where they differ.
