How products are rated
  1. Kosher label in the product data → green check.
  2. Pork or pork-derived ingredient, or one whose animal source isn't stated (plain gelatin, rennet, enzymes, mono- and diglycerides, L-cysteine, glycerin, stearic acid…) → red stop.
  3. Beef, veal, lamb or mutton ingredient → question mark (may not be zabiha).
  4. None of the above → green check.
Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a volunteer database: kosher symbols are often missing and ingredient lists can be outdated. Alcohol isn't checked. Always glance at the package for anything that matters.
