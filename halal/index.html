How products are rated
  1. Pork or pork-derived ingredient → red stop, always.
  2. Halal or kosher label in the product data → green check.
  3. Animal ingredient whose source isn't stated (plain gelatin, rennet, enzymes, mono- and diglycerides, L-cysteine, glycerin, stearic acid…) → red stop.
  4. Beef, lamb or chicken (incl. veal, mutton, turkey) → that animal's icon; check the package for a halal or kosher label.
  5. None of the above → green check.
Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a volunteer database: kosher symbols are often missing and ingredient lists can be outdated. Alcohol isn't checked. Always glance at the package for anything that matters.
